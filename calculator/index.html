How the numbers are worked out

Repayments use the effective monthly rate formula — the balance accrues interest each month at (1 + APR/100)1/12 − 1 and a level payment clears it over the term — the same method behind our loan comparison table and worked examples. Lender rates in the dropdowns are the published rates we verify fortnightly (see the rate tracker), baked in when this page was last built.

Privacy: this calculator is plain client-side JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent, stored, or tracked — close the tab and it's gone.

Results are illustrative and assume a fixed rate and no fees or early-repayment charges. Your offered rate may differ from any advertised rate, particularly with risk-priced lenders. Always confirm current rates and terms directly with the lender. BorrowClever is an independent information service — this is not financial advice.
