What these numbers actually mean

Three things stand out from the table that a rate-only comparison hides.

First: the monthly gap is deceptively small. Between PTSB Personal Loan and Credit union average (ILCU) the difference is €14.36 a month — a couple of coffees. Multiplied across 60 payments it becomes €861, which is real money to hand a lender for nothing extra in return.

Second: "credit union" is not one rate. First Choice Credit Union's 8.84% band rate costs €2,310 in interest here, while the ILCU national average of 10.42% costs €2,734 — a €424 spread inside the credit union world itself. Your own credit union could sit anywhere in that range (legally capped at 12.68% APR), which is why we compare specific confirmed rates rather than the average alone.

Third: Avant's fixed rate buys certainty, not the lowest cost. At 8.50% fixed, Avant Money costs €346 more than PTSB Personal Loan over the term — but the repayment can never rise, unlike the variable-rate bank loans above it in the table. Note Avant prices by credit profile: 8.50% is the representative rate, and their APR can reach 19.9% (see our representative APR worked example).
